Featured case study · Full stack

TicketCRM, an SLA-driven helpdesk

A complete support desk I built end to end: tickets come in by email or the portal, get routed to the right agent automatically, and are tracked against SLAs that respect business hours. Managers get a live analytics view of how the whole operation is doing.

  • Enterprise analytics. Total inflow, resolutions, SLA breaches and MTTR, with volume trends, SLA achievement by priority, an agent leaderboard and channel split, filterable by date, customer and project.
  • SLA engine. Business-hour-aware timers, rule-based auto-assignment and multi-level escalation.
  • Background jobs. BullMQ + Redis run email-to-ticket over IMAP, SLA checks, CSV imports and notifications off the main API.
  • Ops console. Queues, workflows, customers, projects, domain approvals and role-based user admin, with live updates over Socket.IO.
